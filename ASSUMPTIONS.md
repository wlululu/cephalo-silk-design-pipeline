# Final V2 refinement — assumptions

1. Single-image interpretation: 120 m main span, 28 m rib crown, 14 m deck, and two 18 m level approach decks. Hidden structure is mirrored across the span center and transverse centerline.

2. The three V2 chord paths, original station coordinates and section sizes are preserved. Each former 6 m primary rib member is split into three 2 m elements sampled on the same V2 analytic curve; no broadening, tapering or change of crown height is introduced. V2 lacing and diamond cells remain.

3. Four flared root fans extend from the rib ends to broad, discrete foundations 10 m below the deck. Each fan has multiple intersecting strand families with shared mesh nodes, and connects to deck edge girders.

4. The six original V2 side-web panel locations and hubs are retained. Each panel has twelve local spokes rather than six, two existing-scale capture bands with slight radial grading, and fine inter-ring strands. Additional radial branches use tertiary sections; central panels have less fine cross-weave than the shoulders. No global orb, large crescent or new motif is introduced.

5. A continuous 156 m deck surface joins the main span and two level approaches. The protected corridor is 12 m wide with 5 m reserved headroom; these are design assumptions, not regulatory compliance claims.

6. Structural floor members sit below the deck surface. Woven guardrails 1.35 m high run along both deck edges; guardrail meshes are visual nonstructural elements and have no verified vehicle-impact capacity.

7. Main bridge stiffness comes from a frame grillage, woven arches, root fans and web frames. All structural members use bilateral Euler–Bernoulli frame elements with rigid joints, including slender strands; no tension-only cable or prestress model is used.

8. Only the root fan foundation nodes and remote approach bearing nodes are restrained. There are no hidden fixed supports at the central bridge deck ends. Foundation compliance and soil are omitted.

9. Assumed hollow steel sections: E=200 GPa, nu=0.30, fy=355 MPa. Primary scale controls ribs/root spines/deck; secondary scale controls all woven strands and side webs.

10. Deck pressure acts on the 120 m main span only, at tributary edge nodes; approach live load, self-weight, collision and dynamic effects are excluded. The walking surface distributes load but has no shell stiffness.

The final geometry is constrained to the V2 global form. Added arch points sample the same analytic curves. Local web topology is an inference from the single reference image. Crossings are mechanically connected only by shared node IDs. Fine members remain bilateral frames; tension-only cable behavior and prestress are omitted.
